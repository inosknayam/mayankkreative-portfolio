# ⚡ Quick Comparison: Cloudflare vs Vercel

## Which One Should You Choose?

Both are **excellent FREE options** for deploying your Next.js portfolio. Here's a quick comparison to help you decide:

---

## 🏆 The Winner Depends on Your Needs

### Choose Cloudflare Pages if:
- ✅ You expect **high traffic** or want unlimited bandwidth
- ✅ You want **unlimited requests** (no limits at all)
- ✅ You want the **best global performance** (200+ locations)
- ✅ You want **built-in DDoS protection**
- ✅ You might transfer your domain to Cloudflare later
- ✅ You want **better free tier** (truly unlimited)

### Choose Vercel if:
- ✅ You want the **absolute easiest** setup (3 clicks)
- ✅ You want **native Next.js** integration (made by same team)
- ✅ You want **better developer experience**
- ✅ You're okay with 100GB bandwidth/month (plenty for most sites)
- ✅ You want **faster initial setup** (5 min vs 10 min)
